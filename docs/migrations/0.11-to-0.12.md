# Upgrade from 0.11 to 0.12

0.12 replaces the categorical palette and adds a runtime resolver for
renderers. Classes and core tokens are unchanged; the structured changes are
in [`MIGRATIONS.json`](../../MIGRATIONS.json). If you are coming from 0.10,
apply the [0.11 migration](0.10-to-0.11.md) first.

## Review charts that relied on series 1 being the accent

Until 0.11, `--chart-1` was `var(--accent)` and series 2–8 were Okabe-Ito.
From 0.12 the eight slots are fixed hues: blue, orange, aqua, yellow, magenta,
green, violet, red. No slot is the accent.

1. Find uses of `--chart-1`, `range.category[0]`, `charts.<theme>.categorical[0]`
   and `ACCENT` from `@ponchia/ui/charts`. `ACCENT` is removed.
2. Where a chart meant "the brand", paint that one mark with the accent through
   an explicit encoding (`brontoVegaAccent(theme)`, or `var(--accent)` in SVG),
   and leave the categorical palette for categories.
3. Where you pinned `--chart-1` to a fixed value to keep the accent out of
   your data, delete the pin.
4. `brontoVegaAccent(theme)` now returns the resolved `--accent`, and
   `brontoVegaNeutral(theme)` the resolved `--text-dim`. They no longer index
   `range.category`.

## Sequential ramps have five steps

`--chart-seq-6` is removed; `--chart-seq-1..5` is one blue hue, step 1 nearest
the surface. Re-map a six-bin choropleth or heatmap to five bins, or
interpolate between steps in your renderer.

## The static Vega config is frameless

`brontoVegaConfig()` sets `view.stroke: null` (no plot frame), draws a single
series in the first categorical hue, keeps a `--panel` gap between adjacent
rect, arc and area fills, and sets readable label sizes. A spec's own `config`
still wins. Review dashboards that relied on the frame or the accent-coloured
default mark.

## New: categorical identity and runtime tokens

- `--cat-N`, `--cat-N-tint` and `--cat-N-ink` carry the same hues for tags,
  participants and user-chosen tints. Replace hand-picked tag colours, and
  status tokens borrowed as category colours, with these.
- `@ponchia/ui/renderer` resolves the live theme for canvas, WebGL and SVG
  renderers (`readTokens`, `observeTokens`, `vegaConfig`, `xtermTheme`). A page
  that switches skin, contrast or the OLED surface should use it instead of
  `charts.json` or the static Vega files. See [renderer](../renderer.md).
